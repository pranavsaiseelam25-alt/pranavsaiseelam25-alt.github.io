SAGE

Active development

AI · Windows desktop · Local-first

A personal AI assistant that understands a request, carries it out with my approval, and checks that it actually worked. It's an engineering project, not artificial general intelligence.

  • Python
  • FastAPI
  • React
  • Ollama
  • SQLite

Now

  • Phase 1 built: skill routing, diagnostics, and opening apps, files and websites
  • Every action is confirmed and audited
  • 320 automated tests passing, 28 skipped

Next

  • Phase 2: local voice, planned but not started
  • Later: multi-step tasks, web research and mobile
Engineering decisions
  • Local models: private and free to run
  • Speech on the CPU: the 4 GB GPU is kept for the language model
  • Confirmation first: a wrong action costs more than a click
  • Verified results: requested is not the same as succeeded
  • Protected memory: misheard speech can't become false memories
Development journal
  • Routing: fixed inconsistent skill routing
  • False success: stopped made-up success messages with execution checks
  • Files: limited searches to approved folders
  • Tests: 303 to 320 passing
  • Live check: verified the Instagram refusal fix

Runs locally for now, so there's no public link yet

NeuroCube

Early-stage research

Founder and chief project director · Ignite Youth Community

I started NeuroCube to help people through cubing. We're exploring whether cube-based activities could support neurological rehabilitation research.

Now

  • About 50 people involved
  • Research direction set, framework in progress

Next

  • A literature review and testable hypotheses
  • Guidance from neuroscience and rehabilitation experts
Research question
  • How do structured, progressively challenging Rubik's Cube-based activities engage fine-motor coordination, visuomotor integration and executive functions, and what is their potential relevance to complementary cognitive-motor rehabilitation research?

Not clinically validated, and no rehabilitation outcomes yet.

The Mathematics of Chaos

Planned for Grade 11

Proposed research · Theoretical physics

A mostly theoretical study of why a double pendulum becomes chaotic, using mathematics and computer simulation rather than experiments.

Now

  • Topic and research question chosen
  • Research has not started

Next

  • Model the motion with nonlinear equations
  • Simulate how tiny changes in starting conditions grow
Research question
  • How do small variations in initial conditions influence the long-term behavior and predictability of a double pendulum, and how can mathematical modeling explain the emergence of chaotic motion?
